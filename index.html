Our story

Our paths crossed long before we knew it. Peter was studying for an MA in Photography at De Montfort University between 2011 and 2013, while Aga graduated from DMU in Film, Photography and Video Production in 2011. We were quite possibly walking the same corridors.

Then came the second near miss. Over the North Sea, Aga was photographing Typhoons in flight while Peter was photographing the Voyager refuelling them. We were each photographing the other, without a clue.

Third time lucky. In 2016, Aga arrived at RAF Leeming for her trade training and found Peter running the section. This time, we noticed.

Q & A

What should I wear?

Relaxed. Wear something you feel lovely in, and bring a layer, because old cathedrals can be chilly in April.

Can I bring a plus one or children?

It's a small, intimate day, so please check your invitation for who it covers.

Is the Portly Pig close to the cathedral?

Yes, it's a few minutes' walk through the centre of Ripon.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Cathedral rules don't allow guests to photograph or record during the service, so please enjoy the moment and keep your phones in your pockets until afterwards.

Can we throw confetti?

Yes, but only outside and away from the entrance, and only biodegradable confetti or dried petals. The south side of the churchyard is the best spot.
